Phone compasses report magnetic north; the target bearing is true. Around Sarasota magnetic north is ~5° west of true, so the correction is on by default. If the arrow seems about 5° off when you check against a known landmark, toggle it.

Hold the phone flat (screen up) and away from metal, cars and speakers. Do a figure-8 motion if it seems off.

Rockets climb as they go downrange – once you’re facing the bearing, scan from the horizon up to ~30°.
